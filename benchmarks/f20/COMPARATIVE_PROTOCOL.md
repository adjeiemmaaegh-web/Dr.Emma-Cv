# Stage F20 — preregistered Enhancv comparison and blind document study
**Protocol only; no accounts accessed, no test participants recruited, no comparative scores measured.**
Primary public feature sources independently checked 17 Nov 2026:
- https://enhancv.com/resources/resume-checker/ (27 checks across seven published categories)
- https://enhancv.com/features/job-application-tracker/ (online tracker, job data and linked CV versions)
- https://enhancv.com/resources/linkedin-coach/ (16 advertised checks)
- https://enhancv.com/jobs/ (hosted job discovery)
All vendor claims are **self-reported feature descriptions** until tested. No login, scraping, reverse engineering or actual applicant files are authorized for F20 research.

## Preregister and compare equivalent tasks
1. Recruit at least **120 consenting evaluators**, stratified across early, mid/senior, technical/non-technical, and mobile/desktop familiarity. Collect no unnecessary identity attributes.
2. Create **eight fully synthetic, ground-truth profiles** and eight corresponding synthetic job postings (four easy, four difficult) with balanced genders/names solely as fictional source material and remove employer identifiers from outputs.
3. Randomize subjects to order AB or BA, crossover with identical facts and tools, equal time cap. Never alter a fact to make either product look better. Record eligibility/attrition and per-arm missing outcomes. Use lawful owner-provided Enhancv access, not forged credentials.
4. Core tasks: new CV → import an old CV → 30-template equivalent design and A4/Letter PDF/DOCX → job gap analysis → approve factual tailoring → cover letter → exact-version tracker → backup/reload/offline. Mark feature unavailable rather than making up a score.
5. Mask tool branding in exported CVs for **blind independent** recruiters reviewing readability, structure, consistency and role fit. Separate brand-aware UI usability reviewers from brand-blind document judges.
6. Primary outcome: proportion preferring Ledger among forced-choice blinded comparisons, target 65% (study planning objective, **not a result**). Publish numerator, denominator, 95% Wilson CI and ties/nonresponse. Secondary: time-to-completion, objective task success, critical field preservation, wrong-fact rate, keyboard/accessibility failure counts, perceived usability.
7. Reviewers independently label hallucinated credentials/metrics, false positives, layout clipping and missing data; adjudicate disagreements using only supplied evidence. No ATS vendor selection probability is claimed.
8. Record exact version, date, browser, language, device and self-reported account tier for competitor, since features and prices change. Keep any third-party accounts under owner control. Remove local documents and screenshots according to the consent notice.

## Decision rule
A claimed win requires the preregistered outcome threshold, confidence interval, harm/safety comparison and no unresolved release-critical defects. If study not run: **win/tie/lose = UNVERIFIED**. Publishing comparative claims without execution or using Enhancv's own testimonials as independent evidence is forbidden.
